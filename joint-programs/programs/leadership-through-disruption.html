Overview

Organizations operate in a rapidly evolving environment shaped by geopolitical uncertainty, technological disruption, evolving market dynamics, AI-driven transformation, and global supply chain challenges. To succeed, leaders must anticipate change, make informed strategic decisions, and build organizations that are resilient, agile, and equipped to thrive in the face of uncertainty.

Delivered by faculty from three partner schools: Onsi Sawiris School of Business at The American University in Cairo (AUC), Koç University Graduate School of Business, and Suliman S. Olayan School of Business at The American University of Beirut (AUB), this interdisciplinary program is structured across six modules that build the capabilities leaders need to navigate complexity. Participants begin by examining the geopolitical forces and strategic risks shaping the global business landscape before learning how to develop strategies that respond to evolving market conditions.

The program then explores how executives can leverage data and analytics to make informed decisions while leading organizational transformation, culture, and governance in the age of AI. Finally, participants strengthen their ability to build resilient supply chains and apply AI and design thinking to drive innovation and create sustainable value.

Key Takeaways

INTERPRET

Geopolitical shifts and supply-side risks as strategic signals, and assess their implications for markets, investments, and competitive positioning.

DIAGNOSE

Competitive environments and select the strategic approach best suited to each business context.

LEVERAGE

Data and analytics to make informed decisions by questioning outputs, identifying bias, and evaluating the strength of evidence.

LEAD

Organizational transformation through AI by addressing workforce change, governance, ethics, and trust.

STRENGTHEN

Supply chain resilience by assessing geopolitical, supplier, and logistics risks, and balancing cost efficiency with risk mitigation.

APPLY

Design thinking and AI to generate, prototype, and present customer-centered solutions that drive innovation.

Program Outline

Six modules delivered across 11 weeks in a live online format, building an integrated learning journey from geopolitics to innovation.

Module 1 · Koç University

Geopolitics for Business Leaders

Explore the geopolitical forces shaping the global business environment, including great-power competition, geoeconomics, energy security, and technological competition. Learn practical frameworks for assessing risks and opportunities.

3 Hours Live Online
Module 2 · AUC

Choosing the Right Strategy for Your Business Environment

Assess predictability, uncertainty, and an organization's ability to shape its market, then align the appropriate strategic approach. Built on the Strategy Palette framework.

6 Hours Live Online
Module 3 · AUB

Executive Data Management

Cover foundational data concepts, including types, sources, life cycle, and quality dimensions. Learn practical skills to transform numbers into actionable insights and recommendations.

6 Hours Live Online
Module 4 · Koç University

Supply Chain Management and Logistics

Cover supply chain architecture, logistics fundamentals, risks, and a structured framework for building resilient, adaptive networks. Map vulnerabilities and calibrate mitigation responses.

12 Hours Live Online
Module 5 · AUC

Leading in the Age of AI

Explore the strategic impact of AI, the leadership skills needed to drive AI transformation, and participate in an applied workshop addressing a real organizational challenge.

6 Hours Live Online
Module 6 · AUB

Innovate with AI: The Design Thinking Process

Take a hands-on journey through design thinking, showing where AI adds the most value. Empathize, frame, generate, verify, and prototype solutions with people.

6 Hours Live Online

Who Should Attend

Strategy professionals and decision-makers across the MENA region and beyond who operate in volatile, fast-changing markets and seek to sharpen their strategic thinking, identify risks earlier, and lead resilient organizations through disruption.
